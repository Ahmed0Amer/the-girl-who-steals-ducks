How to Play

1. Move & Collect TEREA: Use Arrow Keys, WASD, or the D-pad to collect the 4 TEREA flavors. Finish auto-generated missions to heal +1 ♥ and level up every 2 missions!
2. Power-Ups: Grab a Coffee Cup for a 5s speed boost. Grab 20% XXXX for wavy vision & 2× points on everything you pick up!
3. Steal Ducks (Space): Press Spacebar (or tap Steal Duck) to snatch a nearby duck from a safe distance for +3 pts.
4. Throw Helping Duck (F): Every 5 ducks you defeat unlocks a Helping Duck! Press F (or tap Throw Duck) to send out a golden ally duck that hunts down enemy ducks!
5. Call Ahmed — Boxing Buddy (B): Press B (or tap Call Ahmed) to summon Ahmed! He rushes in with red boxing gloves and lands a 3-hit boxing combo knocking out ducks for +4 pts each!
